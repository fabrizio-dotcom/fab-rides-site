Where We Gather

Portland

Sport climbing on Dorset limestone, with the sea below.

Where
Isle of Portland, Dorset, on the Jurassic Coast
What we do
Sport climbing
Typical grades
6a to 7a
From Winchester
About an hour and a half by car

What it’s like

Portland is a block of limestone tied to the mainland by Chesil Beach. The stone that built St Paul’s Cathedral now gives us sea cliffs and old quarries full of bolted routes.

There are routes for every grade, a big sky, and a sea that changes the mood of the day.

How we climb it

We go as a small group and pick a sector that suits the conditions and the climbers. These days most of our routes sit between 6a and 7a, picked to suit whoever turns up.

Newer climbers are very welcome. Come along with the group, second routes and climb on top rope while you build your confidence.

What to bring

Bring your own harness, helmet and shoes; we share the rest of the kit. Helmets are compulsory on our outdoor days: sea cliffs and old quarries drop loose rock.

Good to know

It’s exposed: sun, wind and salt all play a part. Sea-cliff sectors depend on tide and swell.

Some areas have access agreements or seasonal restrictions. We check the BMC Regional Access Database before we go.

Come along

Sessions and trips are announced in the Signal group. Get in touch and we’ll add you.
