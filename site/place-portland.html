Where
Isle of Portland, Dorset, on the Jurassic Coast
What we do
Sport climbing
When
Day trips when the weather is right, mostly spring to autumn
From Winchester
About an hour and a half by car

What it’s like

Portland is a block of limestone tied to the mainland by Chesil Beach. The stone that built St Paul’s Cathedral now gives us sea cliffs and old quarries full of bolted routes.

There are routes for every grade, a big sky, and a sea that changes the mood of the day.

How we climb it

We go as a small group, pick a sector that suits the conditions and the climbers, and share ropes and beta.

Good to know

It’s exposed: sun, wind and salt all play a part. Sea-cliff sectors depend on tide and swell.

Some areas have access agreements or seasonal restrictions. We check the BMC Regional Access Database before we go.

Come along

Sessions and trips are announced in the Signal group. Get in touch and we’ll add you.
