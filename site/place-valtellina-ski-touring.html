Where We Gather

Valtellina ski touring

Quiet valleys, no queues, and turns you’ve earned.

Where
Valgerola, Val Tartano, Valle Spluga (Italy) and the Maloja Pass (Switzerland)
What we do
Ski touring, plus resort days at Pescegallo, Madesimo and Chiesa in Valmalenco
When
Trips in January and April
Who it suits
Confident intermediate ski tourers with full kit. Not for beginners.

What it’s like

Away from the big resorts, the side valleys of lower Valtellina and the Orobie Alps are wide open in winter. You skin up through larch and pasture, take in the silence at the top, and pick your line down.

Further north, Valle Spluga and the Maloja Pass at the head of Val Bregaglia add high, snow-sure terrain within a short drive.

How we tour

Small groups, early starts, and a plan that bends to the snow. January is for deep winter days; April is for long spring tours on firm snow. For resort days we head to Pescegallo, Madesimo or Chiesa in Valmalenco.

Touring with us is for confident intermediate ski tourers with their full kit. It isn’t for beginners.

Guides, and your own responsibility

Want a guide? We’ll put you in touch with qualified mountain guides: our first choice is Stefano Pizzagalli, and we know the other good ones in the valley.

Or tour with us as part of an experienced group of friends. We aren’t mountain guides, and our tours aren’t guided or instructed. You decide whether the tour, the snow and the terrain are right for you, you can turn back at any point, and you are responsible for your own safety.

You bring your own avalanche kit and know how to use it, and you hold insurance that covers ski touring and mountain rescue abroad. We may ask you not to join a tour, or a day’s group, that isn’t right for you.

Safety

We read the avalanche bulletin every day and choose the route around it. We practise transceiver searches together. Everyone brings full kit in good working order: transceiver, probe, shovel and skins.

Come along

Sessions and trips are announced in the Signal group. Get in touch and we’ll add you.
