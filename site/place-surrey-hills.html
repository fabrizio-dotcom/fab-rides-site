Where We Gather

Surrey Hills

Saturday destination rides on the best singletrack in the South East.

Where
Surrey Hills National Landscape, starting from Peaslake
What we do
Mountain biking
When
Saturdays, all year
From Winchester
About an hour and a half by car. We share cars.

What it’s like

Sandy, rooty singletrack through pine and birch on the greensand hills south of Guildford. The soil drains fast, so it stays rideable when Hampshire chalk has turned to grease.

The trails are technical and varied: short sharp climbs, then long descents that reward good line choice. Leith Hill, the highest point in Surrey, is never far away.

How we ride it

We usually start from Peaslake, the village at the heart of the Hurtwood trails. We carpool from Winchester, ride for a few hours, regroup often, and eat before the drive home.

Cafés and pubs come and go, so our post-ride stop changes too. Got a favourite? Tell us.

Good to know

The Hurtwood is looked after by Friends of the Hurtwood, a small charity that keeps the paths, car parks and public access going with one full-time ranger and donations. We don’t support them yet. If you ride there often, they’re well worth a donation.

Peaslake gets busy on weekends. Arriving early makes parking easier.

Come along

Sessions and trips are announced in the Signal group. Get in touch and we’ll add you.
