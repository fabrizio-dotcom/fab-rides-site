Where We Gather

Fab Rides doesn’t have a headquarters. It has a geography: a loose, moving landscape of car parks, crags, trail heads, mountain huts, and the occasional exceptional pub.

Winchester, Home Base

Winchester is where most of us start. The city sits at the edge of good terrain: 40 minutes from Queen Elizabeth Country Park, an hour and a half from the Surrey Hills and from Portland. For rides, we meet at the QECP car park, or at the Battery in Oliver’s Battery or Fab’s Cave for local loops in West Wood. For climbing, it’s Parthian in Southampton on Monday, Wednesday and Friday evenings.

More about riding from Winchester →

After most sessions: either the pub nearest the trailhead, or coffee somewhere on the way back. The debrief is non-negotiable.

South East England

MTB · Hampshire

Queen Elizabeth Country Park

Our local anchor. Flow trails, pump track, and enduro descents. The Friday morning default and regular Sunday venue. Car park meetup, confirmed per ride via Signal.

MTB · Surrey

Surrey Hills

Saturday destination rides. Box Hill, Leith Hill, Holmbury: serious singletrack in beautiful woodland. Carpools arranged from Winchester. One of our most-visited spots.

The Alps, Valtellina Base

The Alps aren’t a destination we visit occasionally. They’re part of how Fab Rides thinks and moves. We have a base in Valtellina, near Lake Como, and in the Dolomites, which anchors our seasonal community trips.

MTB + Climbing

Valtellina and Val di Mello

Over 50 MTB routes in the valley and the peaks around it. Three kinds of rock: granite in Val di Mello, the purple-red Verrucano of Valgerola, and limestone at Lake Como. Trips from Easter to October.

Skiing

Valgerola / Val Tartano / Spluga / Maloja

World-class skiing without the overcrowded resorts. January and April community trips. Ski touring terrain that rivals anywhere in the Alps.

More about Valgerola →
Climbing + MTB + Skiing

Dolomites

Pale di San Martino. Tre Cime di Lavaredo. Iconic terrain for multi-day climbing and enduro riding. Where the adventures scale up.

Climbing + Hiking

Lake Como

Limestone climbing and multi-pitch around Lecco, the Sentiero del Viandante along the shore, and walks that end at good restaurants above Dervio, Bellano and Varenna.

Beyond Europe

Fab Riders go further when the right opportunity comes together. In 2025, a group of us raced Wines2Whales in South Africa: three days of MTB through the Winelands. The organisers, the culture, the people were all exceptional. We’re building the foundation to return stronger, this time with a bigger Winchester and Dolomites contingent.

International trips happen when the right group and the right event align. If you’re interested in future adventures, get in touch.

After the Ride

Half the relationship-building happens after the ride. The trail is the opening; the rest is the conversation.

The pub stop, the coffee, the glass of wine at the end of a long day in the mountains. These aren’t afterthoughts. They’re part of what makes Fab Rides what it is.
