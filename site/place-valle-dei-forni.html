Where We Gather

Valle dei Forni

Some of the best ski touring in Europe, above Santa Caterina.

Where
Valfurva, Upper Valtellina, in the Stelvio National Park
What we do
Ski touring
When
During winter trips
Who it suits
Confident ski tourers with full kit

What it’s like

Above Santa Caterina, the road ends and the Valle dei Forni opens towards the glaciers of the Ortles-Cevedale group. It holds some of the best ski touring routes in Europe. That’s what brings us here.

How we tour here

Same rules as all our ski touring: full kit, the avalanche bulletin first, and everyone makes their own call.

Come along

Sessions and trips are announced in the Signal group. Get in touch and we’ll add you.
