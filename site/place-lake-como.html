Where We Gather

Lake Como

Limestone multi-pitch, the Wayfarer’s Path and long lunches above the lake.

Where
Lombardy, Italy: Lecco and the eastern shore of the lake
What we do
Limestone climbing and multi-pitch, walking
When
All year: Galbiate and Piani d’Erna in winter, Versasio and Nibbio in summer
Getting there
About an hour from Milan

What it’s like

Steep limestone mountains rising straight out of deep water. The Sentiero del Viandante, the Wayfarer’s Path, follows the eastern shore on old trade routes from Abbadia Lariana to Colico.

Lecco

Lecco is a climbing town with a long tradition, and its limestone is where we do our multi-pitch. Our focus now is Galbiate and Piani d’Erna in winter, Versasio and Nibbio in summer.

How we use it

A stage of the Viandante, a day of climbing around Lecco, or a good walk that ends at a restaurant above Dervio, Bellano or Varenna. Lake Como days fit neatly around a Valtellina trip.

Come along

Sessions and trips are announced in the Signal group. Get in touch and we’ll add you.
