What it’s like

Graded walls under a roof. It’s where we keep strength and technique going through the winter and where most new climbers start with us before moving outside.

How we climb here

Sessions are relaxed. We warm up together, pick problems or routes to work on, and swap beta. First-timers get a proper briefing and a partner who knows the ropes.

Good to know

You can hire shoes at the wall. Entry is paid at the desk.

Come along

Sessions and trips are announced in the Signal group. Get in touch and we’ll add you.
