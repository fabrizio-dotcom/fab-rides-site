Where We Gather

Livigno

High, cold and snow-sure, with bikes once the snow has gone.

Where
A high valley in the Lombard Alps, close to the Swiss border
What we do
Off-piste and piste skiing; mountain biking in summer
When
Day trips from Valtellina
Who it suits
Intermediate and advanced skiers

What it’s like

Livigno sits at around 1,800 metres, so the snow arrives early and stays late. It hosted the freestyle and snowboard events of the 2026 Winter Olympics.

How we use it

Plenty of off-piste, good piste days when the group wants them, and mountain biking once the snow has gone.

Come along

Sessions and trips are announced in the Signal group. Get in touch and we’ll add you.
