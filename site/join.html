Join the Community

No membership form. No subscription. No performance standard. Just show up, ride your own ride, and see if this feels like your kind of people.

How It Works

Fab Rides runs on a Signal group (we don’t use WhatsApp), word of mouth, and the field notes that go out every Friday. When a session is happening, it’s announced. You join if you can. Simple.

Costs, kept honest

Fab Rides isn’t here to make a profit. When a day or a trip has real costs, we share them fairly: travel and logistics, the courses and qualifications that keep us safe, and the time it takes to plan things properly. Covering those costs keeps Fab Rides going. Creating value for the people who come along is the reason we do it.

Who Joins

Families

Adventure Seekers

Ready to go bigger? Alpine trips, international races, multi-day touring. The community has a broad horizon.

Your First Session

  • Tell us it’s your first time. We’ll brief you before we set off.
  • No one gets dropped. We ride together, regroup, wait at junctions.
  • Bring appropriate kit. See the Ride, Climb, and Ski pages for specifics.
  • Plan to stay for coffee or a pub stop after.
  • Ask questions about routes, gear, technique. We like talking about this stuff.

Get in Touch

The simplest way to join is to message directly. No application process, no waitlist. Just a conversation about what you’d like to do and when the next suitable session is.

Email fab@fab-rides.co.uk with what you’d like to do (ride, climb, ski) and we’ll add you to the Signal group.
