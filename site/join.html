Until the end of January

Rebuilding season

Every mountain year has its quiet months, and this winter is ours. Until the end of January, personal and work commitments come first, and we’re taking time to recover and rebuild.

For now there’s no regular schedule: climbing sessions stay small, rides are fewer, and the miles are slower. Some days will still happen, in small groups and at short notice. Message us to hear what’s planned.

The full rhythm returns in February.

How It Works

Fab Rides runs on a Signal group (we don’t use WhatsApp), word of mouth, and the field notes that go out every Friday. When a session is happening, it’s announced. You join if you can. Simple.

Taking Part

Mountain biking, climbing, scrambling and ski touring carry a risk of injury, and in the mountains, worse. Everyone who joins us takes part at their own risk and is responsible for their own safety, kit and decisions. Please be honest with us about your experience and fitness, so we can suggest the right day.

Fab Rides days are informal. We share skills and experience as friends; none of it is professional guiding or instruction unless we tell you so in writing. Children take part with a parent or guardian who stays responsible for them.

Make sure you’re insured for what you plan to do, including mountain rescue when we’re abroad.

Costs, kept honest

Fab Rides isn’t here to make a profit. When a day or a trip has real costs, we share them fairly and openly: travel, accommodation, guides when we use them, and the training that keeps us safe. You’ll know what something costs, and what that covers, before you commit. Covering those costs keeps Fab Rides going. Creating value for the people who come along is the reason we do it.

Your First Session

  • Tell us it’s your first time. We’ll brief you before we set off.
  • No one gets dropped. We ride together, regroup, wait at junctions.
  • Bring appropriate kit. See the Ride, Climb, and Ski pages for specifics.
  • Plan to stay for coffee or a pub stop after.
  • Ask questions about routes, gear, technique. We like talking about this stuff.

Get in Touch

The simplest way to join is to message directly. No application process, no waitlist. Just a conversation about what you’d like to do and when the next suitable session is.

Email fab@fab-rides.co.uk with what you’d like to do (ride, climb, ski) and we’ll add you to the Signal group.
