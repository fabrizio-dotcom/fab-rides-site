Climb

Indoor walls and outdoor crags. Building strength, technique, and trust, one move at a time.

Why We Climb

Climbing strips everything down to essentials. You, the rock, and the problem in front of you. There’s no hiding from your weaknesses. No shortcuts. Just honest feedback about where you are and what you need to work on.

It’s also deeply social. Belaying requires trust. Beta-sharing builds connection. Celebrating sends creates community. We climb together because it makes us better, as climbers and as people.

The Journey

I’m currently climbing at 6c onsight and 7a+ redpoint, working toward 7b onsight as a 2026 goal, with 8a redpoint on the longer horizon. I’m also pursuing my International Mountain Leader qualification. I share this not as credentials but as context: I’m on a journey too, and you’re welcome to join wherever you are on yours.

Whether you’re touching plastic for the first time or projecting your hardest grade, there’s space here.

Session Types

Indoor Sessions

Regular sessions at Southampton climbing wall. Building strength, technique, year-round regardless of weather. Friday mornings are the usual slot.

Dorset coast

Swanage

Coastal limestone with something for every grade. Beautiful setting. A community favourite for outdoor days.

Alpine Climbing

Multi-pitch routes in the Dolomites and Valtellina. Longer days, bigger exposure, deeper rewards. Occasional community trips for experienced climbers. The Dolomite limestone is unlike anything in the UK.

What to Expect

  • Supportive atmosphere: No ego, no pressure. Try hard, fall off, try again.
  • Mixed abilities: Everyone finds something to work on.
  • Gear: Indoor, hire shoes at the wall if needed. Outdoor, let us know what you have.
  • Beginners absolutely welcome: We get you climbing safely from session one.
