Where We Gather

Valtellina

Our Alpine base: mountain biking, three kinds of rock and long days from Easter to October.

Where
Lombardy, Italy: the valley of the Adda, from Lake Como up to Bormio
What we do
Mountain biking; climbing on granite, Verrucano and limestone
When
Trips from Easter to October
Getting there
Fly to Milan or Bergamo, then about two hours by car

What it’s like

Valtellina runs east from the top of Lake Como into the high Alps. The sunny slopes are stepped with terraced vineyards; the shaded side is forest, pasture and side valleys that climb fast.

There are more than fifty mountain bike routes in the valley and the peaks around it, from old mule tracks to proper Alpine descents.

Three kinds of rock

Val di Mello, a short drive up Val Masino, is a nature reserve of granite slabs, boulders and clear water: friction, cracks and big walls.

Valgerola has something rarer: the purple-red Verrucano Lombardo, a conglomerate you won’t find at home.

And down at Lake Como, limestone and multi-pitch above Lecco.

Where to stay

Stay close to Fab’s base in Sacco, in Valgerola. We usually point people to Agriturismo Ortesida, near Morbegno. A friend’s place in Dossa is coming soon: details to follow.

How we use it

Our Valtellina season runs from Easter to October. Trips mix riding and climbing days, with evenings that move slowly.

Those terraces grow the Nebbiolo behind the valley’s wines. Taste them through Fab Wines.

Come along

Sessions and trips are announced in the Signal group. Get in touch and we’ll add you.
