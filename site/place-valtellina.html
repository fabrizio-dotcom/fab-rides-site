Where We Gather

Valtellina

Our Alpine base: mountain biking, granite and long summer days.

Where
Lombardy, Italy: the valley of the Adda, from Lake Como up to Bormio
What we do
Mountain biking, multi-pitch and granite climbing in Val di Mello
When
Community trips in summer, typically late summer
Getting there
Fly to Milan or Bergamo, then about two hours by car

What it’s like

Valtellina runs east from the top of Lake Como into the high Alps. The sunny slopes are stepped with terraced vineyards; the shaded side is forest, pasture and side valleys that climb fast.

There are more than fifty mountain bike routes in the valley and the peaks around it, from old mule tracks to proper Alpine descents.

Val di Mello

A short drive up Val Masino, Val di Mello is a nature reserve of granite slabs, boulders and clear water. It’s a different kind of climbing from the limestone at home: friction, cracks and big walls.

How we use it

This is where Fab Rides feels most at home. Summer trips mix riding and climbing days, with evenings that move slowly and a glass of the valley’s own wine.
