Where We Gather

Bormio

Big vertical, a historic town and our main resort base in January.

Where
Upper Valtellina, Lombardy, on the edge of the Stelvio National Park
What we do
Resort skiing, off-piste when conditions allow, ski touring
When
January trip
Who it suits
Confident on red runs, minimum

What it’s like

An old town of stone and frescoes under a long, steep mountain. The Stelvio piste is one of the great downhill courses, and in 2026 Bormio hosted the men’s alpine events and the first Olympic ski mountaineering races.

How we use it

Bormio is our main resort base in January. We ski as a group, stop for a proper mountain lunch, and split into smaller groups when the snow invites it.

When the legs ask for it, we finish in hot water: the Bagni Vecchi, the Bagni Nuovi, or a natural hot spring hidden in a nearby valley that we only share on the trip.

Come along

Sessions and trips are announced in the Signal group. Get in touch and we’ll add you.
