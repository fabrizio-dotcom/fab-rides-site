Where We Gather

Wines2Whales, South Africa

Three days of mountain biking from the Winelands to the coast.

Where
Western Cape, South Africa: from the Winelands near Somerset West to Hermanus
What we do
Mountain bike stage racing, in teams of two
When
We raced it in 2025
Who it suits
Riders ready for three long days in the saddle

What it’s like

Wines2Whales crosses the mountains of the Western Cape through vineyards, fynbos and forest, and finishes in Hermanus, famous for its whales.

The organisers, the culture and the people were exceptional.

What’s next

Going back is a dream, this time for the “other” race, the big one. It takes months of preparation, so here’s what we’ve learned so far.

Build the base early. Long, steady rides through the winter, then back-to-back days so your body learns to recover overnight.

Train with your partner. You race as a team of two: learn each other’s pace, when to push and when to wait.

Ride technical trails on tired legs. The hardest descents tend to come late in the day.

Practise eating on the bike. Little and often, with the food you’ll race on.

Get the bike right. A full service well before, tyres you trust, and spares you know how to fit.

If that sounds like your kind of challenge, start riding with us now and tell us.
