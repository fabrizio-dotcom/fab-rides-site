Where We Gather

Dolomites

Where the adventures scale up.

Where
North-east Italy: Pale di San Martino, Tre Cime di Lavaredo and around
What we do
Multi-pitch climbing, enduro riding, skiing
When
Every year, May to July
Getting there
Fly to Venice or Verona, then two to three hours by car

What it’s like

Pale walls of dolomite rising out of green meadows, a UNESCO World Heritage landscape. The Pale di San Martino above San Martino di Castrozza, and the three towers of the Tre Cime di Lavaredo, are among the most recognisable mountains in the Alps.

How we climb here

Multi-pitch days are a group effort, and only once we’ve climbed together and know each other’s skills. We don’t usually hire guides for these days, unless they come along as friends.

Weather

We plan to be off exposed ground from early afternoon until about five, when storms tend to build. We keep an eye on temperature and pressure through the day, and we avoid descending by via ferrata when a storm could arrive.

Come along

Sessions and trips are announced in the Signal group. Get in touch and we’ll add you.
