Ski

Winter adventures in the Alps. From resort days to backcountry ski touring.

Mountains in Winter

There’s nothing quite like the Alps in winter. The scale. The silence. The way a mountain transforms under snow. Whether you’re carving groomers, exploring the backcountry on touring skis, or finding fresh lines off-piste, winter in the mountains is adventure in its purest form.

What We Offer

Resort Days

Group days at resorts in Valtellina and the wider Alps. Skiing together, stopping for mountain lunches, sharing the experience. All abilities, from confident intermediates to expert skiers.

Ski Touring

For those wanting to go deeper: earn your turns through the backcountry. Skin up, ski down, find your own lines. Requires fitness, avalanche awareness, and appropriate gear. Smaller groups, bigger adventures.

UK Sessions

When Scotland delivers, we’ll head north. Occasional dry slope sessions for off-season practice and pre-trip preparation.

Our Valtellina Base

Our Alpine base gives access to world-class skiing without the crowds. Bormio, Livigno, Santa Caterina, all within reach. Plus ski touring terrain that rivals anywhere in the Alps.

Resort + Touring

Bormio

Long vertical drop, great piste variety, and serious off-piste when conditions are right. Our main resort base for January trips.

Resort

Livigno

High altitude resort with reliable snow. Good for intermediate and advanced skiers. Accessible from Valtellina for a day trip.

Quieter resort

Santa Caterina

Less crowded alternative to Bormio. Excellent terrain, World Cup downhill pedigree. Great for a focused day on the mountain.

Earn your turns

Backcountry Touring

Alpine touring terrain across the Valtellina valley. Requires touring setup and avalanche safety gear. Smaller groups, bigger rewards.

Community trips typically run in January and March.

What You Need

  • Resort skiing: Confident on red runs minimum. Own gear or hire locally.
  • Ski touring: Good fitness, touring setup, avalanche safety gear (transceiver, probe, shovel). Some backcountry experience preferred.
  • All trips: Travel insurance covering winter sports. Flexibility for weather windows.
